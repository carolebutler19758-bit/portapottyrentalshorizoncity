Frequently Asked Questions

How do you ensure service quality across different regions in the U.S.?

We utilize a standardized 10-point sanitization protocol at every one of our national hubs. This means that whether you are in a major city or a rural area, the unit quality and cleaning standards remain identical.

What is included in the porta potty rental cost?

Our quotes are all-inclusive, covering the initial delivery, a set number of sanitization and restocking visits, and the final removal of the equipment. We believe in total pricing transparency with no hidden fuel or environmental surcharges.

Can you handle long-term industrial contracts?

Absolutely. We specialize in long-term rentals for construction and industrial sites, providing a fixed maintenance schedule that ensures your facilities stay compliant and fresh for months or even years.

What is the "1/4 rule" for maintenance?

Our internal goal is to service every unit before the waste tank reaches 25% capacity. This proactive approach prevents odors and ensures the unit is always ready for high-volume use without the risk of overflows.

Do you provide ADA-compliant units nationwide?

Yes. We maintain an extensive inventory of wheelchair-accessible units that feature ground-level entry and reinforced grab bars to ensure your site meets all legal accessibility requirements.
